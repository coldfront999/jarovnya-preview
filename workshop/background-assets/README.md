# Background and type study

The owner selected background 2 (urban) and background 3 (poster) on 14 October 2026. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using the supplied restaurant concept collage as the reference. These are background assets, not replacement food photos or logos. Original PNG exports are saved under outputs/backgrounds/ in the task workspace; source generated images remain under the tool's generated_images directory. WebP files are mobile exports of those images.

## Urban prompt (verbatim)

Edit the supplied restaurant app concept collage into ONE clean portrait background asset inspired specifically by panel 3 URBAN DARK. Output only background, no phone, no UI, no logo, no lettering, no text, no food, no buttons. Deep matte charcoal black tangible fine mineral / charcoal paper, barely visible gritty grain and subtle worn print texture. A sparse gestural vermilion orange flame contour drawn with dry ink at bottom left and very small orange brush flecks near top right; accents live at the outermost edges, not center. At least 75% of central and upper area nearly uniform charcoal suitable for readable cream UI text. The tone is authorial street-food screenprinted artwork, not gamer wallpaper or glossy lava. No glowing neon, no gradient, no smoke cloud, no photoreal fire, no tiled pattern, no slogans, no crown. Flat full-bleed portrait 2:3 artwork. Ignore all other collage panels.

## Poster prompt (verbatim)

Edit the supplied restaurant app concept collage into ONE clean portrait background asset inspired specifically by panel 7 NEOBRUTALISM. Output only background artwork, no phone, no interface, no letters, no text, no logo, no food, no buttons. Warm white uncoated paper with a genuine low-contrast fine printed-paper texture. A ripped rough-edged orange vermilion screenprint block intrudes along the bottom-right edge, forming an irregular diagonal occupying only bottom-right 20%; sparse fine black halftone dots within that orange corner and around its torn boundary. A tiny torn orange strip at upper-left margin ties the composition together. Upper 70% and center remain calm pale paper for black UI text. A distinct tactile rebellious food-poster print surface, controlled, deliberate, high contrast at corners only, no generic gradients, no shapes floating in center, no perfect geometry. Full bleed portrait 2:3, isolate this direction only from supplied collage.

## Font sources

Oswald, Caveat, Russo One and Golos Text are self-hosted from the official google/fonts repository, each with its accompanying SIL Open Font License. Exact original filenames: ofl/oswald/Oswald[wght].ttf, ofl/caveat/Caveat[wght].ttf, ofl/russoone/RussoOne-Regular.ttf, ofl/golostext/GolosText[wght].ttf. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts. All four families declare Cyrillic support in their official METADATA.pb files.

The study is isolated under workshop/. It does not create orders, register a service worker, or change the stable three-theme preview.
